# Dola AI Video Watermark Remover — User Guide

**Dola AI Video Watermark Remover & Downloader** automatically intercepts and downloads **100% Watermark-Free 1080p Raw MP4 videos** from Dola AI and Doubao.

---

## 1. How to Install / Reload in Chrome

1. **Open Extensions Page**:
   - In Google Chrome (or Brave / Edge / Opera), navigate to: `chrome://extensions/`
2. **Enable Developer Mode**:
   - Toggle on **Developer mode** in the top-right corner.
3. **Load / Reload Extension**:
   - Click **Load unpacked** and select this extension folder (or click the reload icon 🔄 if already loaded).
4. **Pin to Toolbar**:
   - Click the extension icon in Chrome's top bar and pin **Dola Downloader**.

---

## 2. How to Use

1. **Auto-Download (Default)**:
   - Ensure the **Auto-Download Videos** toggle is **ON** in the extension popup.
   - Go to [dola.com](https://dola.com) or [doubao.com](https://doubao.com) and generate any video.
   - The second the video finishes generating, the extension intercepts the master 1080p stream and saves the video **without watermarks** into your `Downloads/Dola_Videos/` folder.

2. **1-Click Screen Grab**:
   - Open the popup and click **Download Video on Screen** to immediately grab any completed video on your active tab.

3. **Custom Destination Folder**:
   - Update the folder name in the popup (e.g. `My_Dola_Videos`) and click **Save**.
   - Review saved items under **Download History** and click the folder icon to open the file location directly on your computer.
